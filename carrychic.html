Our Philosophy

The Art of Slow Fashion & Pure Artisan Craft

At Carry Chic, we believe fashion shouldn't be rushed. In an era of mass-produced uniformity, we celebrate the intimate beauty of items created slowly by hand with passion, dedication, and precision.

Founded by Farah Talaat, our brand brings together timeless aesthetics and contemporary silhouettes. We source pure cotton cords, organic weaves, and brass fittings that age gracefully with you.

Zero Mass Production Strictly micro-batch and made-to-order releases.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our handcrafted process, care, and delivery.

How long does it take to make and deliver a bag?
In-stock pieces ship within 24 to 48 hours. Bespoke or customized orders require approximately 5 to 8 business days to handcraft from scratch before dispatch.
Can I request a custom color or monogram?
Yes! We love bespoke projects. You can select custom cord shades, hardware finishes (gold, brass, silver), and custom hand-stitched monograms. Use our contact form below or reach out via WhatsApp.
How do I clean and care for my handmade bag?
We recommend gentle spot cleaning with a damp cloth and mild eco-friendly detergent. Store your bag inside the provided luxury dust bag stuffed with tissue paper to preserve its sculptural shape.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept Cash on Delivery (COD), InstaPay, Mobile Wallets, and Credit/Debit Cards. Orders can also be confirmed directly via WhatsApp.
